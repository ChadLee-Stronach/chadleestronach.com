Chad Lee-Stronach

Assistant Professor of Philosophy, Northeastern University

Email · CV · PhilPapers · Google Scholar · ORCID

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Philosophy and Religion at Northeastern University, Boston.

My research is in ethics and decision theory, with related interests in political philosophy, philosophy of economics, formal epistemology, and the ethics of artificial intelligence. Much of my recent work examines how problems of ethics and justice depend on information: who possesses it, how it is accessed and controlled, and what it is about. I am currently writing a monograph, Systemic Justice.

In 2025, I was a Visiting Fellow in the School of Philosophy at the Australian National University. In 2023–24, I was a Joint Fellow-in-Residence at Harvard University, held jointly with the Edmond & Lily Safra Center for Ethics and the Center for Research on Computation and Society. Before joining Northeastern, I was an Interdisciplinary Postdoctoral Fellow at the Center for Ethics in Society, Stanford University, with a placement at Apple University. I received my PhD in Philosophy from the Australian National University in 2019.
